1. What we provide

SteadyHandle is a done-for-you service. We set up, train and manage an AI phone agent for your business. The agent answers calls in your business name, day or night, answers common questions, and takes down who called and what they need so you can call them back. It does not book jobs on a calendar, text a summary of the call, text callers back, send follow-up messages, answer chats on your website, or connect the caller to you during the call. Included minutes and prices are the ones shown on our pricing section or quoted to you in writing. A line marked "coming soon" on that page is not part of the service yet.

2. How the AI agent works

Calls are handled by an automated AI agent, not a human. The agent tells callers it is an AI assistant for your business, takes down their details, and ends the call. AI systems can misunderstand, mishear or give an incomplete answer. We test your agent before launch, review calls and keep tuning it, but we cannot guarantee that every call will be handled perfectly. You should review your calls and tell us about anything that needs to be corrected.

The agent does not provide medical, legal, financial or other professional advice, and it is not a substitute for your professional judgment.

3. Not for emergencies

SteadyHandle is not an emergency service. The agent cannot contact emergency services or dispatch help. Anyone with a medical, fire, safety or other emergency should hang up and call 911 or their local emergency number.

4. Text messaging (SMS)
