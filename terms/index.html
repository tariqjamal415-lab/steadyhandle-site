1. What we provide

SteadyHandle is a done-for-you service. We set up, train and manage an AI phone agent for your business. Depending on the plan you choose, that agent answers calls, responds to website chats, books appointments on your calendar, texts back callers who hang up, sends you summaries of conversations, transfers urgent calls to you or your on-call person, and sends confirmations, reminders and follow-ups. The specific features, included minutes and prices are the ones shown on our pricing section or quoted to you in writing.

2. How the AI agent works

Conversations are handled by an automated AI agent, not a human. The agent tells callers it is an AI assistant for your business, and callers can ask to be connected to a person. AI systems can misunderstand, mishear or give an incomplete answer. We test your agent before launch, review conversations and keep tuning it, but we cannot guarantee that every conversation will be handled perfectly. You should review your call summaries and tell us about anything that needs to be corrected.

The agent does not provide medical, legal, financial or other professional advice, and it is not a substitute for your professional judgment.

3. Not for emergencies

SteadyHandle is not an emergency service. The agent cannot contact emergency services or dispatch help. Anyone with a medical, fire, safety or other emergency should hang up and call 911 or their local emergency number.
