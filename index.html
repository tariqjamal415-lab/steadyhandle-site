FAQ

Common questions

Will callers know they're speaking with an AI?

Yes. Your agent introduces itself as an AI assistant for your business. Callers get a fast, friendly answer instead of voicemail, and the agent takes down their details so you can call them back.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Keep your existing number and send calls to your agent when you can't answer or after hours.

What happens with emergencies or urgent calls?

The agent takes down who called and what they need, then ends the call, including when the caller says it is urgent. It cannot contact emergency services. Anyone with a medical, fire, safety or other emergency should hang up and call 911.

Will it work for my type of business?

If customers call you to ask questions or request service, yes. The agent answers in your business name and takes down who called and what they need so you can call them back. We tailor it to your industry, whether that's a service visit, a cleaning, a consultation or a haircut.

Can it handle medical or legal calls?

The agent can answer general questions and take down who called and what they need. It does not give medical or legal advice. Practices that need HIPAA-compliant handling should mention it on our call so we can set things up correctly.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live in about 7 days.

Is there a contract?

No. All plans are month-to-month. You can cancel at any time.

What if the agent gets something wrong?

We review conversations every week and keep improving your agent's answers. You can also flag any call and we'll update it quickly.
