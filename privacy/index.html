Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information SteadyHandle collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers our website at steadyhandle.com, the people and businesses who buy our service, and the callers who speak with an AI phone agent that we set up and run for a business customer.

1. Who we are

SteadyHandle is a done-for-you AI phone answering service for local businesses, founded by Tariq Jamal. We set up, train and manage an AI agent that answers a business customer's calls in that business's name, answers common questions, and takes down who called and what they need so the business can call back.

For our own website visitors and business customers, SteadyHandle decides how information is used. When our AI agent handles calls for a business customer, we act on that business's behalf and follow their instructions — that business's own privacy practices also apply.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Your name, business name, email address and phone number when you book a call, email us, or sign up.
  • Details about your business that we need to build your agent: services, hours and the answers you want callers to hear.
  • Billing contact details. Payments are handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.

Information from calls and our website chat

  • Caller phone numbers, the time and length of a call, and whether the call was answered or missed.
  • Audio recordings and transcripts of calls handled by the AI agent.
  • What the caller tells the agent — for example a name, callback number, service address and a description of what they need.
  • Messages typed into the chat assistant on the SteadyHandle website, and any contact details shared there, such as a name, business name, phone number or email address. We use these to answer the question and to reply if you asked us to.

Information collected automatically

  • Standard server and log data from our website host and service providers, such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested. Our website does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3. Text messaging and mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with or sold to any third parties or affiliates.

The phone agent does not text callers after a call, send appointment confirmations, reminders or other follow-up messages, or text the business a summary of a call. We do not share mobile phone numbers, or text-message opt-in and consent data, with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.

4. Call recording and transcription

Calls handled by the AI agent may be recorded and transcribed so that the business can review what was said, so the caller's details are captured accurately, and so we can improve how the agent answers. Recordings and transcripts are made available to the business customer whose calls the agent is answering.

The AI agent identifies itself as an AI assistant for the business. Our business customers are responsible for providing any recording notice their state or industry requires; we can configure the agent to announce that calls are recorded.

5. How we use information

  • To answer calls in a business customer's name, answer common questions, and take down who called and what they need so the business can call back.
  • To answer questions sent through the chat assistant on the SteadyHandle website.
  • To set up, train, test and tune a customer's agent, including reviewing conversations for quality and correcting mistakes.
  • To provide support, send service and billing notices, and respond to your questions.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fraud, and meet our legal obligations.

We do not use the contents of our customers' calls or messages to send marketing to their callers.

6. How we share information

We share information only in these situations:

  • With the business whose calls we answer. Call details, recordings and transcripts go to that business.
  • With service providers who process data on our behalf so the service can work — for example telephony and AI voice providers, the provider for the chat assistant on the SteadyHandle website, email delivery, our website host, and our payment processor. They may use the information only to perform services for us.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of SteadyHandle, our customers or the public.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or sale of assets, in which case this policy continues to apply to the information transferred.

7. We do not sell your information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing. As stated above, this specifically includes mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in and consent data, which are never shared or sold for marketing purposes.

8. How long we keep information

We keep account and billing records for as long as you are a customer and afterwards as needed for our records, taxes and legal obligations. Call recordings and transcripts are kept for as long as the business customer needs them for their records, and we delete them on request unless we are required to keep them.

9. Security

We use reasonable safeguards such as encrypted connections, access controls and limiting who can see customer data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your choices and requests

  • Email and marketing: you can unsubscribe from our emails using the link in the message or by emailing us.
  • Text messages: the phone agent does not text you about calls. If you ever receive a text from SteadyHandle, reply STOP to opt out, or HELP for help.
  • Access, correction and deletion: email us and we will respond. Depending on where you live you may have the right to request a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to certain uses. We will not discriminate against you for making a request.

Send privacy requests to tariq.steadyhandle@mail.grokbot.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

11. If you called a business that uses SteadyHandle

When you call a business that uses our service, we handle that call for the business. The business decides what happens with the information, and their privacy policy governs it. If you contact us with a request about a call, we will share your request with the business and help them respond.

12. Children

Our service is meant for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, email us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will tell our customers directly.

14. Contact us

Questions or privacy requests: tariq.steadyhandle@mail.grokbot.com.
